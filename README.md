# Bioluminescent Blobs

**A generative deep-sea organism piece by Dr. Bill Kolomyjec.**

## Description

Four translucent, cilia-fringed blobs drift and orbit through a 100,000-particle bioluminescent swarm suspended in a deep-hued void. Each blob carries its own glowing nucleus and a membrane that catches light like wet tissue, and the whole scene breathes: as two blobs drift close to one another, their color intensifies, flaring brighter before fading back down as they part. A slow, organic twisting field warps the swarm and the blobs' cilia in unison, and real-time bloom post-processing blooms every hot spot into a soft glow. The color palette — a triad of hues spaced evenly around the wheel — is re-randomized on every load, so no two visits look quite the same.

Built with [Three.js](https://threejs.org/), rendering 100k+ vertices in pure GLSL.
